# NSFC Proposal Section Writing Guide

## Official Proposal Structure

The NSFC proposal follows this mandatory structure:

### Part I: Rationale and Research Content
1. **Project Rationale (1. 项目的立项依据)**
   - 1.1 Research Significance (研究意义)
   - 1.2 Domestic and International Research Status (国内外研究现状及发展动态)
   - 1.3 Key Scientific Problems (拟解决的关键科学问题)

2. **Research Content, Objectives, and Key Scientific Problems**
   - Research Content (研究内容)
   - Research Objectives (研究目标)

3. **Research Plan and Feasibility Analysis**
   - Research Methods and Technical Route (研究方案、技术路线)
   - Key Technologies (关键技术)
   - Feasibility Analysis (可行性分析)

4. **Characteristics and Innovations (本项目的特色与创新之处)**

5. **Annual Plan and Expected Results (年度研究计划及预期研究结果)**

### Part II: Research Foundation
- Research Foundation and Feasibility (研究基础与可行性分析)
- Working Conditions (工作条件)
- Ongoing Related Projects (正在承担的相关科研项目)
- Completed NSFC Projects (完成国家自然科学基金项目情况)

### Part III: Other Notes

## Section-Specific Writing Requirements

### 1.1 Research Significance (研究意义)
- **Length**: 350-550 characters
- **Structure**: 4 continuous paragraphs (no numbering, no subheadings):
  1. Macro background / industry status / global challenge (~100-120 chars)
  2. Current treatment / research bottlenecks (~100-120 chars)
  3. Current research progress + clear research gaps (~120-180 chars, most important)
  4. Significance and innovation of this study (~100-120 chars)
- **Citations**: 8-15 papers, bracket numbered [1-3], [4,5]
- **Style**: Formal, academic, objective. No colloquialisms, no adjective stacking

### 1.2 Research Status (国内外研究现状)
- **Length**: 3-5 paragraphs, each >= 600 characters
- **Each paragraph** must contain 3-5 sub-topics in natural prose (not listed)
- **Subheadings**: Must be domain-specific, concept-clear (6-18 chars), e.g., "14-3-3亚型依赖的基质力学调控缺口"
- **Forbidden subheadings**: Generic ones like "关键科学问题", "研究热点与空白"
- **Citations**: Each paragraph must cite 6-10 papers, embedded in specific conclusions
- **Content**: Only research status, not significance, not "this project will..."
- Format: `2.1 [subheading]\n(paragraph text)\n\n2.2 [subheading]\n(paragraph text)`

### 1.3 Key Scientific Problems (关键科学问题)
- **Count**: Exactly 3 problems
- **Length**: 35-60 characters each
- **Must start with**: 揭示 / 阐明 / 解析 / 辨识 / 界定 / 构建
- **Must simultaneously satisfy**:
  - Derived from gaps identified in research status
  - More abstract than research objectives (no task attributes)
  - Highly consistent with project title and direction
- **Forbidden**: "this project will...", experimental methods, model names, questions
- **Format**: (1) 揭示...\n(2) 阐明...\n(3) 解析...

### Research Objectives (研究目标)
- **Structure**:
  - Overall objective: 1 paragraph, <= 80 characters
  - Objective 1/2/3: each <= 60 characters
- More specific than scientific problems, but still no methods
- **Forbidden**: "through...method", "using...technique", experimental steps

### Research Content (研究内容)
- Count must match research objectives exactly, one-to-one correspondence
- Each item: 120-160 characters
- Start with "拟..." (拟解析, 拟阐明, 拟构建)
- Science-level description, not experimental steps

### Research Plan and Methods (研究方案)
- Detailed technical route for each research content
- Include specific experimental designs, model systems, detection methods
- Write in logical order following the technical route diagram

### Innovation Points (创新点)
- 3-4 points covering: theoretical innovation, methodological innovation, application innovation
- Each must clearly state what is innovative relative to existing work

### Feasibility Analysis (可行性分析)
- Technical feasibility: methods and tools are available and proven
- Team foundation: relevant expertise and preliminary results
- Resource conditions: equipment, collaborations, samples

### Annual Plan (年度研究计划)
- By year, with milestone-style planning
- Year 1: Foundation setup and initial experiments
- Year 2: Core experiments and data collection
- Year 3: Integration, validation, and publication

## Writing Style Standards
- Formal academic Chinese, objective tone
- Based on scientific facts and logical reasoning
- No colloquialisms, no adjective stacking, no slogans
- Citations use sequential bracket numbering [1-3], [4,5]
- Never fabricate literature or cite non-existent papers
- Use provided literature pool for all citations
